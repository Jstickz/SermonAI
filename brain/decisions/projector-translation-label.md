# The projector shows the translation as a small corner abbreviation

**Decided:** 16 October 2026 by the operator · **Parked since:** 21 September 2026 · **Branding:** §15.2 keeps the projector minimal

The third projector layout ("verse, reference and translation") shows the translation's short name (KJV, NIV) small, muted, monospaced, in the bottom-right corner: readable from the booth, ignorable from a pew. Full attribution (the copyright line YouVersion requires wherever text is shown) stays off the congregation's screen and belongs on the operator screen and in the summary PDF.

Why: §15.2 splits the obligation into a short version label on the projector and the full line in the PDF. Until the PDF renderer exists (M4), the obligation is met by storage: every cached verse stores its attribution and the store refuses a verse without one. Consequence that still holds: no licensed translation should be projected in front of a congregation until the PDF carries the line.

Also decided for the renderer: blank or empty is canvas-coloured, not black, so the projector's own "no signal" is never mistaken for a blank; the only violet is a 2px rule under the reference; no logo.
