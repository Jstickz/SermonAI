## What happened

- **Bundled translations: KJV, WEB and ASV** (FR-59). WEB from YouVersion; KJV and ASV from ebible.org, because YouVersion licenses no King James to the key and returns no copyright string for its ASV. 3.86 MB. Each pack ships a `.sha256` and a manifest with source, licence and attribution; the builder refuses a pack whose per-book counts disagree with the source.

- macOS floor kept at 12+ and the reasoning written down (testing one version shows that version works, not that older ones fail).
- **M0 closed: 12 of 12 deliverables, 6 of 6 DoD lines.**
- M1 began: `audio/devices.rs` enumerates inputs, loopback and virtual cables (FR-01, 02, 05); `audio/capture.rs` and `audio/convert.rs` capture at 16 kHz mono in 250 ms chunks with `rubato` band-limiting the resample (FR-03). Verified on the 4-channel 48 kHz mic array.

## Decisions

- Loopback format must be read from the *output* config on Windows; `supported_input_configs()` is empty for an output device. macOS has no loopback at all, so output devices are not offered there.
- Resample properly, never decimate: dropping two of three samples folds a 12 kHz sibilant into a 4 kHz tone and passes every obvious check.
