# 2026-09-09 (Tuesday): offline semantic search in Rust

**Milestone:** M0 deliverable 8. **Commit:** 7bddf84 (01:52, so effectively the end of the 8 September session).

## What happened

- `detection/vector.rs` loads the encoder and the int8 index, replicates model2vec's pooling exactly, and searches by int8 dot product. Verbatim quotes retrieve themselves, which proves the Rust queries share the Python-built index's space.
- Latency 2.46 ms per query against FR-15's 5 ms, after two fixes: `opt-level = "s"` had made it 15.98 ms, and the dot product was widened to 8 accumulators.
- Verified on macOS Intel and Apple Silicon by CI run 34296856629 (recorded 16 September, [[sessions/2026-09-16]]).

Lesson kept in [[lessons]]: a size-optimised profile can cost a hot loop six times its speed; measure the budget in the release profile.
