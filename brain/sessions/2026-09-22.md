## What happened

- Input picker, then the **level meter** at 30 fps from the raw device buffers (not the 250 ms chunks), peaks held between frames, three tiers (green to −12 dBFS, amber to −3, red above) at the operator's request. UI realigned to `docs/wireframe.html`.
- **Transport** start/stop/pause/resume without reopening the device (FR-06). Pause holds the device. Stop flushes the converter tail: 1.98 s captured from a 2 s run, where it was 1.75 s.
- **Deepgram Nova-3 streaming** (FR-07), verified live: 14.8 s sample, 12 interims, 3 finals, exact transcript. `TranscriptEvent` is two variants, not a struct with `is_final`, because a live run showed "...if you will to join" become "...to John chapter three". Bounded channel with `try_send` so the audio thread never blocks; KeepAlive every 8 s; CloseStream and drain on stop.
- **Keyterms** (FR-09): 66 books from `CANON` plus 30 archaic terms, books first so a trim loses typos before verses. The A/B against synthesised speech was a null result: text-to-speech articulates too well to need the bias.
- Transcript events to the Live tab (FR-10); `start_capture` takes a `transcribe` flag so a Settings device test never opens a billed connection.
- Transcript and rolling 60-second buffer moved into Rust (FR-11), window measured from the newest segment's end, interim text excluded.
- Live Transcript Panel: auto-scroll that turns itself off on scroll-back, paragraphs from pauses of 2.5 s or more, three font sizes.
- Reconnect with backoff 0.5 s to 30 s, never gives up, audio held through the outage. **Bug found live:** the stream clock offset advanced per chunk instead of per stream, so 18.6 s of audio reported timings to 33.8 s.
- Lag measurement and the M1 DoD tooling added; device-loss detection and `switch_capture_device`.
- Hybrid key model written into PRD §17 and §11.3, and the `credentials/` abstraction every client goes through ([[hybrid-key-model]]). `.env` loads in development builds only.
