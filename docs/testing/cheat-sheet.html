SermonAI — keys for the service

Every key works with no text box selected. If a box has the cursor, click anywhere empty first.

Card
a verse the app heard → Staged
you read it once more → Live
on the projector

Every service

EnterThe next step. Nothing staged: stage the top card. Something staged: send it live.
Shift+EnterTop card straight to the projector.
EscClear the staged verse. The projector keeps what it has.
→ ←Next or previous verse of the passage on the projector.

Finding a verse

Ctrl+KSearch any verse. Type john 3 16. Enter stages it, Shift+Enter sends it live, Tab changes translation, Esc closes.
PPin the top card. Pinned verses stay all service, at the top of the list.
1 … 9Send pinned verse 1 to 9 to the projector, any time, as often as needed.

If something is wrong

BBlank the projector at once. Press B again to bring the verse back. Nothing is lost.
EscWrong verse staged? Clear it. It never reached the screen.

Before the service: Live tab → choose the input → switch Transcribe on. Cards appear on the right as scripture is spoken; a card nobody touches leaves after 30 seconds. Auto-live off means nothing reaches the projector without you.
