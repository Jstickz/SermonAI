The Story God Wrote Before You Were Born

Pastor Emeka Nwosu·7 Sept 2026·47 minutes·Faith Foundations, Week 3

Introduction

Before God ever asked you to trust him with your future, he was already writing the story. Emeka opens the sermon in Jeremiah 29, not as a promise about prosperity, but as a reminder that God's plans were already in motion long before Israel could see the outcome.

Main Point 1 · God writes before we see

For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the Lord, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end. Jeremiah 29:11 · KJV

The word "thoughts" here — machashabah — carries the sense of a plan that has been carefully designed. Israel is in Babylon. From their vantage point, the story is over. From God's vantage point, he is already writing the next chapter.

Main Point 2 · The story includes the pain

And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose. Romans 8:28 · KJV

Paul doesn't say all things are good. He says all things work together. The season you're in right now is a paragraph in a larger book. Don't judge the book by the paragraph.

Discussion questions
  1. Where in your life right now does the story feel unfinished?
  2. Emeka said "a paragraph is not the book." What paragraph are you tempted to judge as the whole book?
  3. Read Jeremiah 29 in full. What does verse 10 tell us about God's timeline?
  4. Romans 8:28 has a condition — "to them that love God." Why does that condition matter?
  1. Name one place where you've seen God "work things together" in hindsight.
  2. Trust and control often trade places. Which one wins in you this week?
  3. What does it look like practically to submit a plan to God this week?
Prayer points
  • For the person in the room who cannot see the end of their story right now.
  • For trust in the Author, not just interest in the plot.
  • For the courage to submit our plans to be edited by God this week.
  • For a right memory of past faithfulness that fuels present faith.
